Fair Play Terms

The rules that make every game full. Short, clear, no fine print.

💳

The hold is a promise, not a payment. Your card is pre-authorized — never charged — when you RSVP. The hold is released automatically when you check in.

⏱️

15-minute grace period. Running late? You get a 15-minute grace period after kickoff to check in.

📍

Two-proof check-in. Check in with the QR code or the field geofence, then the organizer confirms. Two proofs, zero arguments.

🎁

No-show fees reward the reliable. No-show fees don't disappear: 85% is split as app credit among the players who checked in, 15% keeps FUTBOL running.

⚖️

24-hour dispute window. Marked as a no-show by mistake? You have 24 hours to dispute it — the organizer reviews every case.

⭐

Your record follows you. Your reliability score lives on your profile. Show up, build trust, get picked first.

↩️

Free cancellation window. Plans change — cancel from the app before the free-cancellation cutoff shown on each game and your hold is released instantly.
